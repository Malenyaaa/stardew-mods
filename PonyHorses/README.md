# Pony Horses

Turn your horse into one of the six main ponies from *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic*:
Twilight Sparkle, Rarity, Rainbow Dash, Fluttershy, Pinkie Pie or Applejack.

## Use

Ride your horse or stand next to it and press **P** (changeable in `config.json` or Generic Mod Config Menu).
Pick a pony or "Normal horse". Every horse can be a different pony.

- Ponies don't wear hats. A hat the horse wore is given back when it becomes a pony, and you can't put a new one on.
- Only the horse's owner can change it.
- The choice is saved with the horse. In multiplayer, players without the mod see a normal horse.
- Tractor Mod's tractor is left alone.

## Sprites

The sprites are based on the pony pixel art by **ponett** and are not part of this repository.
They are built from the source frames with a separate script (walk in-betweens, grazing, tail swish,
carrot munching, ground shadow).
